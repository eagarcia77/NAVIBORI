# NAVIBORI XR — Brand Foundation

## Status
Creative direction approved. The working product name remains subject to formal trademark clearance before legal registration or claims of exclusivity.

## Positioning
**NAVIBORI XR**  
Puerto Rico Spatial Experience Platform

**Pilot 001**  
Mercado Metropolitano de Juana Díaz

## Brand idea
Puerto Rico + navigation + spatial discovery + immersive technology.

## Core visual language
- **Ocean Blue** — navigation, trust and technology
- **Island Teal** — exploration, growth and nature
- **Borinquen Coral** — culture, energy and action
- **Sand Gray** — structure, clarity and neutral surfaces

## Mascot
The approved creative direction uses an original technology-forward Puerto Rican coquí as the NAVIBORI guide.

The mascot is a functional product character, not decoration:
- onboarding guide
- AI spatial concierge
- AR wayfinding companion
- VR guide
- accessibility assistant
- family-friendly discovery character

Working internal designation: **NAVIBORI Coquí Guide**. A final mascot name requires separate clearance.

## Logo system
Required production variants:
1. Primary mark + NAVIBORI XR wordmark
2. Horizontal mark
3. Symbol-only navigation pin
4. Simplified app icon
5. Monochrome accessibility/signage variant

## Product language
Primary: Spanish (Puerto Rico)  
Secondary: English

## Messaging
Primary concept: **Explora. Descubre. Vive Puerto Rico.**  
Secondary concept: **Tu isla. Más cerca.**

## Accessibility rules
- Never communicate state using color alone.
- Maintain WCAG 2.2 AA contrast targets.
- Provide reduced-motion and non-XR alternatives.
- Mascot interactions must never block navigation or essential information.
