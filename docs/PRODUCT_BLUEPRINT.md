# NAVIBORI XR — Product Blueprint v1.0

## Vision
Transform physical destinations in Puerto Rico into intelligent, navigable, accessible and immersive digital experiences.

## Pilot
Mercado Metropolitano de Juana Díaz is Pilot 001. The product must remain municipality-agnostic and multi-venue from the first production release.

## Core experience
Open → locate → discover → select → route → arrive.

## Canonical spatial model
Organization → Municipality → Venue → Building → Floor → Zone → Space → POI → Asset

Routing:
- route_nodes
- route_edges
- accessibility attributes
- temporary closures
- indoor/outdoor classification

## Product engines
### Maps
Interactive 2D/3D map, POI, categories, events, promotions and services.

### Wayfinding
Graph-based routing with standard and accessible route profiles.

### AR
QR-first positioning, AR discovery and wayfinding. BLE/NFC may be added after pilot validation.

### VR
Browser/mobile/headset-compatible exploration powered by the same spatial data.

### Twin
Canonical digital representation for geometry, spaces, POI and assets.

### AI
Spatial concierge represented by the NAVIBORI coquí guide. AI should produce map actions and journeys, not only text.

### Pulse
Privacy-aware aggregated analytics.

### Studio
Spatial CMS for authorized municipal and venue administrators.

## Privacy and safety
- Anonymous navigation must remain possible.
- Precise location requires explicit permission.
- Minimize retention of individual location history.
- Emergency routing must never be generated by unvalidated AI logic.
- SafeRoute remains disabled until approved plans and procedures are supplied.

## Accessibility
Target WCAG 2.2 AA and include:
- keyboard operation
- VoiceOver/TalkBack support
- scalable text
- reduced-motion support
- non-color status cues
- accessible route profile
- non-AR/VR alternatives

## Pilot acceptance criteria
1. Visitor can open the app without an account.
2. Visitor can identify or manually select a validated starting point.
3. Visitor can search verified Mercado Metropolitano POI.
4. Visitor can generate a route to a selected POI.
5. Accessible routing excludes documented barriers.
6. Administrators can update POI without source-code changes.
7. Merchants can update only their own approved content.
8. All spatial edits create an audit trail.
